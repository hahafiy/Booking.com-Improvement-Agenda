# Booking.com Extension Stay Improvement Assistant

An Agentic AI assistant that helps guests to extend their stay and handle their any unavailable rooms in real time.

## Problem Statement
Guests using the Booking.com app often face uncertainty after they book a room, particularly around check-in readiness and whether they can extend their stay once they are already at the property. Today, extending a stay usually means calling the front desk or making a new booking, and room availability is not clearly communicated, so guests can be left with a dead end when a room is no longer free. This project proposes improving the experience from arrival to departure through real-time room availability updates and a timed extension feature. Guests would receive reminders based on the length of their stay and be able to extend up to 24 hours before check-out, depending on availability. The goal is to reduce guest frustration and front desk workload while helping hotels fill rooms that might otherwise sit empty.
